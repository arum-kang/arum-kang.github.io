About

I am an assistant professor of linguistics at Chungnam National University. My research lies at the intersection of formal semantics and pragmatics, with a focus on how natural language encodes and conveys meaning across discourse. I work primarily on Korean, Mandarin Chinese, and Korean Sign Language.

My current work addresses three interconnected questions: how articleless languages like Korean and Mandarin track definiteness and discourse reference; how rhetorical questions update discourse commitments and trigger inference; and how mood, modality, and factivity interact in embedded environments. I also pursue corpus-based and computational approaches to these phenomena, including LLM evaluation of pragmatic inference.

I received my Ph.D. in Linguistics from the University of Chicago (2015).
