Research Profile

My research focuses on the semantics and pragmatics of Korean, particularly modalized questions, disjunction, mood, complementizers, definiteness, and rhetorical meaning. I also work on corpus-based approaches to semantic and pragmatic inference, theoretically informed evaluation of language models, and Korean Sign Language semantics.

Ph.D. in Linguistics, University of Chicago (2015).
