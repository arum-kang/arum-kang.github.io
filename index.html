About

I am an assistant professor of linguistics at Chungnam National University. My work lies at the intersection of theoretical semantics, pragmatics, and their interfaces, with a focus on definiteness, anaphora, modality, rhetorical questions, and mood in Korean, Mandarin Chinese, and Korean Sign Language. I am also interested in computational approaches to meaning, including corpus-based analysis and deep learning methods applied to semantic phenomena.

Ph.D. in Linguistics, University of Chicago (2015)
