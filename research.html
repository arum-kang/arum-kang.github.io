Research

Interrogative Semantics, Modality & Inference

My research investigates the interaction of modality, speaker inference, and question semantics in Korean, with particular focus on modalized questions, disjunction, inquisitiveness, and nonveridical equilibrium.

Recent work extends this approach to mood selection, embedded questions, concessive constructions, and rhetorical inference.

Selected papers
Kang, Arum and Suwon Yoon (2020). From Inquisitive Disjunction to Nonveridical Equilibrium: Modalized Questions in Korean . Linguistics 58(1): 207–244.
Kang, Arum and Sanghoun Song (2025). Mood Selection in Korean Embedded Questions. Language 50(3): 835–861.
Kang, Arum (2025). Factivity Shift and Modal Spread in Korean -un kes-i anil-kka. Studies in Generative Grammar.

Definiteness, Quantification & Reference

My research on definiteness examines how articleless languages encode reference and anaphora without overt articles, with particular attention to bare nominals, demonstratives, and domain restriction.

A central topic concerns the Korean marker KU, which I analyze as a domain restrictor rather than a canonical determiner. Recent work extends this approach to donkey anaphora, quantification, and coreference.

Selected papers
Kang, Arum (2021). Marking Definiteness in an Articleless Language: The Role of the Domain Restrictor KU in Korean . Language and Linguistics 22(2): 301–336.
Park, Myung-Kwan and Arum Kang (2024). Anaphoric Definites without a Determiner. Journal of Cognitive Science 25(1): 67–124.
Kang, Arum (2023). Definiteness in Korean Bare Nominals and Donkey Sentences. Language 48(1): 75–97.

Rhetorical Questions & Discourse Inference

Another major area of my work concerns rhetorical questions and speaker-oriented inference. I analyze rhetorical questions not simply as information-seeking expressions, but as discourse moves that update speaker commitments and restructure discourse expectations.

Recent work examines discourse governance, polarity focus, rhetoricality, and commonsense reasoning in Korean rhetorical questions using formal semantic and corpus-based approaches.

Selected papers
Kang, Arum (2025). Conditions on Exclamation and Polarity Focus in Korean Rhetorical Questions. Language Research 41(1): 7–25.
Kang, Arum (2024). A Study on Rhetorical Questions in Korean: From the View of Verum Focus. Language and Linguistics 105: 1–30.
Kang, Arum (2018). Negative Politeness in Korean Questions. Language Research 54(2): 229–247.
Supported by the National Research Foundation of Korea (2025–2027)
